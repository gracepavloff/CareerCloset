CareerCloset

Professional clothing should open doors, not close wallets.

On-campus interview and career-fair clothing rental

Customer problem

Students are expected to look ready before they can afford to be ready.

The expense

A suit or several business-professional outfits can cost more than a student can justify for a few events each semester.

The consequence

Students improvise, skip opportunities, or carry avoidable financial stress into interviews and career fairs.

Customer hypothesis: college students interviewing for internships and full-time positions.
