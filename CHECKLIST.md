# CareerCloset Pitch 2 Checklist

## Before presenting

- [ ] Confirm the team is in a Pitch 2 Team under People, with no more than four students.
- [ ] Replace planning assumptions with real research only when documented.
- [ ] Run the live deck from `index.html` and test arrow-key navigation.
- [ ] Time the main pitch at four minutes or less.
- [ ] Time the instructor response at 45 seconds or less.
- [ ] Practice the 30-second recommendation and transition.
- [ ] Put the shareable live-deck link in the common Arena queue.

## Numbers audit

Every number is either labeled as an assumption, a target, or a formula:

- Observed evidence: none yet for CareerCloset; do not present zeros as traction.
- Current cash: `$0` planning assumption.
- Ask: `$15,000` planning assumption.
- Net burn: `$2,500/month` planning assumption.
- Runway: `6 months`, calculated as `$15,000 / $2,500`.
- Use of funds: `$6,000 + $2,000 + $4,000 + $3,000 = $15,000`.
- Valuation: `$135,000` cap and `10%` scenario are financing assumptions.
- Milestone: `30` interviews, `75` rentals, one paid sponsor or campus, and `80%` on-time returns are targets.
- Fit gate: `85%` satisfaction is a go/no-go target.
